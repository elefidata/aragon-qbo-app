Your QuickBooks connection has been removed

Aragon Books AI no longer has access to this QuickBooks company until an administrator reconnects it.

If you were directed here after disconnecting the app in QuickBooks or Intuit, the connection between Aragon Books AI and your QuickBooks Online company has been ended. The app cannot read or update that company file until it is authorized again.

What we do after a disconnect

  • We stop using the Intuit connection to access the company file.
  • Access tokens issued for that connection are no longer used and are removed from the company computer that ran the app, or are left unusable once Intuit has revoked them.
  • Local working files already on company devices (for example daily reports) stay under the company's control and are not published.

How to reconnect

Only an authorized administrator at Aragon Group Inc can reconnect the app:

  1. On the company computer that runs Aragon Books AI, open the app and start its QuickBooks authorization step.
  2. Sign in to Intuit with the Aragon Group Inc account and approve access on Intuit's consent screen.
  3. Return to the app so it can store the new connection and resume categorizing the company's books.

This website cannot reconnect the app for you. If you did not mean to disconnect, contact an administrator at Aragon Group Inc.
