Aragon Books AI

An internal QuickBooks companion used only by Aragon Group Inc to keep the company's own books categorized and filed.

Aragon Books AI is a private, company-only application. It is not a public product, is not offered to customers or third parties, and is not listed in an app store. Authorized staff at Aragon Group Inc (Calabasas, California; NMLS 1975617; CA DRE #02092690) run it on company devices to review the company's own QuickBooks Online transactions, tag them as business or personal, file them into the company's profit-and-loss accounts, and produce a daily summary.

What this launch page is for

Intuit requires a public launch URL for the QuickBooks Online app used to connect the company file. If you arrived here from Intuit, you are looking at the information page for that internal connection. The app itself runs on company computers; this site does not sign you in or display any financial data.

Connecting QuickBooks

A company administrator starts the connection from the app on a company computer and signs in with the Aragon Group Inc Intuit account. After Intuit's consent screen, access is limited to the scopes needed to read and update the company's own books.
