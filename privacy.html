Privacy Policy

How Aragon Books AI uses QuickBooks data for Aragon Group Inc's own books.

This policy describes how Aragon Group Inc (the “Company,” Calabasas, California) handles information when its internal application, Aragon Books AI, connects to the Company's QuickBooks Online company file. This is not a consumer app and is not offered to the public.

Information we access from QuickBooks

With the Company's authorization, the application may read and, where needed to file a transaction, update data in the Company's own QuickBooks company, including:

  • Company name and related company settings (for example, class tracking)
  • The chart of accounts and classes
  • Transactions (such as expenses, deposits, bills, and transfers), including payees, amounts, dates, memos, line items, and categories
  • Vendor history used to recognize a merchant
  • Names of receipts or other attachments on a transaction, when present

The application does not request access to other customers' QuickBooks companies. Bank-feed items that have not yet been added to the books may not be visible through Intuit's API.

How we use that information

QuickBooks data is used only to identify what each of the Company's transactions is, tag it as business or personal, file it into the Company's profit-and-loss accounts and classes, keep a local review queue, and prepare a daily summary for Company staff. We do not use this data for advertising, profiling of third parties, or any purpose unrelated to the Company's own bookkeeping.

Where information is stored

Connection tokens, local ledgers, and reports are stored on Company devices (the computers that run the application), not on this public website. This website contains only these policy pages and does not collect QuickBooks data or sign-in information.

AI processing (DeepSeek)

To categorize a transaction, the application may send transaction details (such as payee, memo, line items, account names, and similar context) to the Company's AI model provider, DeepSeek, so the model can suggest what the transaction is and which category and class to use. Those suggestions are reviewed against the Company's rules and confidence thresholds before anything is written back to QuickBooks. We do not sell this information to DeepSeek; it is processed as a service provider for categorization only.

Sharing and sale

The Company does not sell QuickBooks data and does not share it with third parties for their own purposes. We share data only with:

  • Intuit / QuickBooks, as needed to operate the connection the Company authorized
  • DeepSeek, as described above, for categorization
  • Service providers who host or operate Company systems, if any, under a duty to protect the data
  • Professional advisors (for example, a CPA) or authorities when the Company is legally required to do so

Retention

Tokens and working files are kept on Company devices for as long as the Company uses the application, and are removed or become unusable when the QuickBooks connection is disconnected or the Company deletes them. Intuit also controls data retained in the QuickBooks company file itself.

Security

Access to the application is limited to authorized Company staff. The QuickBooks connection uses Intuit's OAuth process. This public site does not store credentials or company financial data.

Your choices

A Company administrator can disconnect the app at any time in QuickBooks or Intuit, or by removing the stored connection on the Company computer. See the disconnect page for what happens next and how to reconnect.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the application, contact Aragon Group Inc in Calabasas, California.
